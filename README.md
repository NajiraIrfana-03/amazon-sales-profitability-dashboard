# Amazon Sales & Profitability Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales & Profitability Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes e-commerce sales, profitability, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, regional trends, returns, and future sales forecasts.

The project transforms structured sample datasets into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data modelling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Evaluate sales target achievement and year-over-year growth.
- Identify top-performing products and customers.
- Understand customer retention and repeat purchase behaviour.
- Analyze regional sales and profitability.
- Monitor product returns and refund amounts.
- Explore future sales trends using forecasting.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Measures, calculated KPIs, and time intelligence.
- **CSV Files** – Dataset source.

## Dataset Description

The project uses seven Amazon-inspired sample CSV datasets:

- Sales
- Customers
- Products
- Regions
- Calendar
- Targets
- Returns

*Note: These are sample datasets created for educational and portfolio purposes. They are not official Amazon business data.*

## Data Modelling

A structured data model was developed to connect sales, customer, product, regional, calendar, target, and return information.

The model supports cross-filtering and business analysis across the dashboard pages.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales & Business Performance
- Overall sales and profit KPIs

### 2. Product & Customer Intelligence

### 3. Regional, Returns & Future Performance
- Regional sales and profitability
- Return quantity and return analysis
- Refund amount and returned order metrics

- Future performance trends

## Key Power BI Features

- Star schema-based data modelling
- Power Query data transformation
- Advanced DAX measures
- Time intelligence calculations
- Interactive slicers and filters
- KPI cards and analytical charts
- Sales target analysis
- Customer retention analysis
- Sales forecasting with confidence intervals

## Business Value

This dashboard helps users understand business performance, identify sales and profitability trends, evaluate customer behaviour, monitor returns, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Project Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic and designed for learning and portfolio demonstration.
- Forecasting results are estimates based on historical sample data.
- Business insights should be interpreted within the context of the sample dataset.
